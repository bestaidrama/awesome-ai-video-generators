## Where to start

Three editorial picks for someone who has never made a series with AI. They are an editor's choice, not a ranking; everything below is listed alphabetically.

1. [SceneMixer](https://scenemixer.com/) — Script or novel in, multi-episode series out: the text is parsed into cast, locations, props and episodes, every character gets a reference sheet, every episode a storyboard, every segment a video clip (Wan 3.0 by default, other tiers selectable), and the clips are assembled into downloadable episodes. Dialogue is spoken in 17 language options; 16:9 by default with 9:16 selectable. 23 public sample series and per-step pricing are on the site.
2. [Runway](https://runwayml.com/) — AI image and video platform with its own Gen-4.5 / Aleph models plus third-party models, an agentic creative collaborator and project workspaces.
3. [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) — Google's AI filmmaking tool built around Veo for creating clips and assembling them into scenes and stories with consistent elements.

## Open-weight video models

Models with published weights, as recorded on [NodeValid](https://nodevalid.com/) from the vendors' own repositories and model cards.

- [Allegro](https://huggingface.co/rhymes-ai/Allegro) — Allegro publishes a 175M autoencoder beside a 2.8B transformer under the apache-2.0 tag, and states single-GPU memory twice: 9.3G with CPU offload switched on, 27.5G with it off. Licence: apache-2.0.
- [CogVideoX](https://github.com/THUDM/CogVideo) — CogVideoX is the one family here whose licence changes with the model size: the 2B model is Apache 2.0 and the 5B carries a separate licence of the project's own. Licence: Split: CogVideoX-2B is Apache 2.0 while CogVideoX-5B carries a separate CogVideoX licence.
- [Cosmos Predict 2.5](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos-Predict2.5-2B) — Cosmos Predict 2.5 fills every cell: the NVIDIA Open Model License with a sentence saying models are commercially usable, 2,059,174,912 parameters, a requirement of 32.54 GB of GPU VRAM, and 720P video at 16FPS delivered as a 5-second clip. Licence: NVIDIA Open Model License, with the card stating that models are commercially usable.
- [FramePack](https://github.com/lllyasviel/FramePack) — FramePack states its memory floor together with what the floor buys: to generate a one-minute video at 30fps, 1800 frames, using the 13B model, the minimal required GPU memory is 6GB. Licence: Apache 2.0.
- [HunyuanVideo](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo) — HunyuanVideo publishes weights for a model of over 13 billion parameters under a licence the vendor wrote itself rather than a standard permissive one. Licence: tencent-hunyuan-community, a vendor-written licence rather than a standard permissive one.
- [LongCat-Video](https://github.com/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video) — LongCat-Video publishes 13.6B parameters under the MIT License and states 720p output at 30fps. Licence: MIT License.
- [LTX-Video](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-Video) — Lightricks publishes LTX-Video under Apache 2.0, including a 13B model with distilled and fp8 variants for lower VRAM. Licence: Apache 2.0.
- [MAGI-1](https://github.com/SandAI-org/MAGI-1) — MAGI-1 states hardware as machine shapes rather than memory: H100 or H800 times eight for the 24B model, one RTX 4090 for the 4.5B. Licence: Apache License 2.0.
- [Mochi 1](https://github.com/genmoai/mochi) — Mochi 1 publishes a 10 billion parameter model under Apache 2.0, with a separately stated 362M autoencoder. Licence: Apache 2.0.
- [Open-Sora](https://github.com/hpcaitech/Open-Sora) — Open-Sora publishes an 11B model under Apache 2.0 and states peak GPU memory against the number of cards: 60.3GB at 768px on a single H100 or H800, dropping to 44.3GB across four or more. Licence: Apache 2.0.
- [Pyramid Flow](https://huggingface.co/rain1011/pyramid-flow-sd3) — Pyramid Flow states its output precisely and its cost not at all: 10-second videos at 768p and 24 FPS, with a 384p checkpoint producing 5 seconds at the same rate. Licence: stabilityai-ai-community.
- [SkyReels-V2](https://huggingface.co/Skywork/SkyReels-V2-DF-14B-540P) — SkyReels-V2 publishes 1.3B, 5B and 14B variants under a licence tagged skywork-license, and states peak VRAM for the same 540P output twice: about 14.7GB on the 1.3B, around 51.2GB on the 14B. Licence: skywork-license.
- [Stable Video Diffusion](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-video-diffusion-img2vid-xt) — Stable Video Diffusion publishes 2B params under a licence tagged stable-video-diffusion-community, which sends commercial use to a separate licence page. Licence: stable-video-diffusion-community, with commercial use directed to a separate licence page.
- [Step-Video-T2V](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step-Video-T2V) — Step-Video-T2V publishes 30 billion parameters under MIT, the shortest licence text in this register attached to its largest published model. Licence: mit, the same tag on the repository and on the model card.
- [Wan](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) — The vendor's own GitHub organisation and Hugging Face organisation publish weights for Wan 2.1 and 2.2 under Apache 2.0. Licence: Apache 2.0.
